The idea in one line

I(X;Y) = H(Y) − H(Y|X), which also equals the KL divergence between the joint p(x,y) and the product p(x)p(y). It is zero exactly when X and Y are independent, and unlike correlation it also catches nonlinear dependence. In ML it drives feature selection, information gain in decision trees, contrastive objectives such as InfoNCE, and clustering metrics like normalized mutual information.
