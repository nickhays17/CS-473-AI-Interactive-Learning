3. Use it to classify

Kernel ridge regression finds weights for every training point, then scores a new point by its similarity to all of them. Try the circles with a linear kernel, then switch to RBF.

Under the hood: the model solves (K + λI) α = y, where K holds the kernel score between every pair of training points. Prediction is f(x) = Σ αᵢ k(xᵢ, x). The shading shows f(x); the dark line is where f = 0.

Low γ or low degree gives smooth, simple boundaries. High values chase individual points and overfit. Raising λ calms that down.
